About Tueste

Two friends and one street

Pepe and Ricardo grew up in Córdoba, went off to see the world and came back with the same idea: a small coffee shop on Calle Deanes where coffee is understood, enjoyed and shared. They work with Café Sin Intermediarios, roast here every week and bake their own pastries.
